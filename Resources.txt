Here are the resources and reference link for identity theft for the application:

IdentityTheft.gov:
A government-run resource for victims of identity theft. It offers personalized recovery plans and step-by-step guidance to help you recover from identity theft.

StaySafeOnline.org:
Managed by the National Cybersecurity Alliance, this site provides tips and resources for staying safe online, including protecting personal information and securing devices.

IDTheftCenter.org:
A nonprofit organization that helps victims of identity theft by providing free assistance and prevention tips.

OnGuardOnline.gov:
Offers practical tips to avoid internet fraud, secure your computer, and protect your personal information. It's a valuable resource for online safety education.

ConsumerFinance.gov:
Managed by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, this site provides information on protecting your finances, including steps to avoid identity theft and fraud.

These websites are reliable and provide comprehensive tools, tips, and strategies to stay safe and recover from identity theft. They are especially useful for individuals seeking to enhance their knowledge and security practices.
